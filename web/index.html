One bot.One bad fill.

        
A single trading model sees a signal and fires. It never asks whether the book can take the size, whether the spread just blew out, or whether a second read disagrees. CONSILIUM makes every trade survive four checks first.

      

      

        Thin book · Demo with sample numbers
        
The 3 a.m. breakout.

        
A token jumps 4% on a handful of trades. SCOUT says buy, QUANT agrees. RISK sees the spread at 120 bps and vetoes. A veto cannot be outvoted, so the desk holds. Twenty minutes later the move is gone.

        SCOUT
        
Reads the trend.

        
EMA20 against EMA50 on hourly candles, plus the 24 hour move. Votes BUY, SELL or HOLD and says why in one line.

        
Agent 1 of 4 · direction

      

      

        QUANT
        
Reads the stretch.

        
RSI14 on the same candles. Under 35 it leans buy, over 65 it leans sell. When SCOUT and QUANT point opposite ways there is no trade.

        
Agent 2 of 4 · direction

      

      

        RISK
        
Holds the veto.

        
Market open, spread under 40 bps, real 24 hour volume, hourly volatility under 3.5%. If any check fails RISK vetoes, and nothing outvotes a veto.

        
Agent 3 of 4 · limits

      

      

        EXEC
        
Walks the book.

        
Prices a 250 USDG ticket against the live order book, level by level. Above 50 bps of slippage it blocks. Three of four behind the trade, or it does not happen.

One API.Three doors.

      
The web desk, the app and the terminal all call the same public endpoints. Build your own front end on them.

      

        
GET/v1/marketsSpot markets in USDG with 24h change and volume

        
GET/v1/council/:symbolFour votes, the verdict and each agent's case

        
GET/v1/candles/:symbolHourly candles from Lighter (res=5m to 1d)

        
POST/v1/askQuestion in, streamed answer out (server-sent events)

Does it trade real money?
No. Every fill is paper: the desk prices it against the live order book and records it, but no order is sent.

        
Which AI runs it?
The votes are plain code you can read. Claude writes each agent's case and answers questions; it cannot change a verdict.

        
Do I need an API key?
No. The app uses our desk API by default. Add your own Claude key with consil key if you prefer.

        
Where is my data?
The app keeps your paper ledger in ~/.consilium on your machine. The web desk keeps it in your browser.
